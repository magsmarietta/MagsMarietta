Instructions

Zipper pulls come in two styles depending on your zipper:

Detached Zipper Loop — The zipper slider has an open loop. Simply thread the tag directly through the loop and you're done. No extra hardware needed.

Attached Zipper Loop — The zipper slider has a fixed or closed loop. In this case, you'll need a jump ring. Thread the jump ring through the zipper loop, attach the tag, then close the ring shut.

Slide the aglet over the tip of your cord or shoelace. Once positioned, use a tool to gently press the top edge inward, crimping it down onto the string. Apply even pressure around the full circumference until it holds firm without sliding. Do not over-crimp, instead press just enough to avoid breaking the cord.

To install, simply unlace your shoe to the desired position. Place the lace hardware where you want it to sit, then relace through or around the piece and continue lacing as normal. This works best on flat laces.
